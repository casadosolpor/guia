🏛️ Monuments & town

Fefiñáns Square & Manor

The historic heart of Cambados: 16th-century manor house, San Bieito church and the arched bridge.

📍 View on map

Santa Mariña Dozo ruins

Roofless 15th-century Gothic church with its arches intact, surrounded by a cemetery. A must-see.

📍 View on map

San Sadurniño Tower

Remains of a defensive tower on an islet next to San Tomé. Beautiful at sunset.

📍 View on map

San Tomé fishing quarter

The old quarter of fishermen and shellfish gatherers, with its harbour and low houses.

📍 View on map

Cambados seafront promenade

Waterside walk along the estuary linking Fefiñáns and San Tomé.

📍 View on map

Monte A Pastora · Chapel & viewpoint

Views over the whole estuary. The A Pastora–Umia trail starts here. Galician nights are home to the legend of the Santa Compaña, a procession of lost souls: ask the locals!
